# A Midsummer Night's Chimp

LG-09B proposal r1, 2026-10-15. UNAPPROVED. Original short monkey adaptation for the first permanent Works play. This document does not replace the reader, Library story or demo.

## Exact proposed body

Only the text inside this fence is the canonical body. Encode as UTF-8 without BOM, retain LF between paragraphs and omit the final newline. The title, fence and surrounding document do not enter the hash or bill.

```text
Lanterns glowed across the summer meadow. Pip the monkey carried bananas for dawn. Two fairies trailed him, arguing about which fairy had borrowed which banana.

"Nobody stole breakfast," Pip said. "Food follows whoever holds the basket."

Behind them, sleepy dreamers planned a forest show. One monkey played a mountain. One played moonlight. Their chief wanted quiet, but the mountain kept sneezing.

Pip tried to help. He shook flower dust, hoping actors would learn their lines. Alas, the mountain dreamed of moonlight, moonlight dreamed of bananas, and their chief said that actors needed pajamas.

The fairies giggled when Pip sneezed. Then both felt the basket was their queen. They bowed. The basket said nothing, which seemed quite royal.

"I have mixed things up," sighed Pip.

Then sunshine lit the glade. Pip swept away the dust and told his story. The mountain hugged him. Moonlight hugged him. Their chief asked for breakfast before hearing more lines.

The fairies gave back each borrowed banana. They all shared toast under the lanterns, while Pip said sorry with a grand bow.

Their show began after breakfast. The mountain sneezed again. They clapped, even the fairies, who agreed that mountains needed hankies.

The basket stayed queen.
```
