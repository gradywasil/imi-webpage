## Recommended rule

Free, default Off, Estate count >=1, introduction complete, and one newly acknowledged manual ordinary Printing after this feature is installed. A manual Printing may precede Estate1; its completed eligibility persists through Printing, Imprint and Estate. Never infer it from printing count, awards, lifetime stars or older receipts. Foundation, Estate, challenge entry and automatic Printing cannot earn it. No tier purchase, mastery, new reward or currency.

Use N=30 real minutes, editable integer 5..240. Reject invalid input rather than silently changing it. Every 60 active game seconds, when >=1 star is pending, print only if the estimated next-star time is strictly greater than N. Require a full 300 visible real seconds and positive measured non-reward income. Equality waits. No alternative zero-rate trigger, minimum-run waitgate or premium clock.

Let E be current S.run.earned in actual bananas, C=Economy.cash(500000)=12,500,000 bananas, k=floor(sqrt(E/C)), gap=(k+1)^2*C-E, R=eligible credited bananas / 300 real seconds, estimate=gap/(60*R) real minutes. E is total run earnings, not current wallet or unspent Legacy. Spending does not lower E. Keep the existing star formula even where gifts contribute to E; exclude gifts from R. Use full precision for decisions, rounded display only.

For E=20,000,000, k=1 and gap=30,000,000. A 300-second sample totaling 3,000,000 gives R=10,000 bananas/real second and estimate=50 minutes, so N30 prints when due. A sample of 5,000,000 gives R=16,666.666666666668 and estimate=30 mathematically, so it waits. The recorded floating result is29.999999999999996 and also waits; do not round displayed minutes into the decision. Samples 4,999,975 and 5,000,025 give about 30.00015000075 and 29.99985000075 respectively, on opposite sides of the strict boundary. At E=49,999,975 and R=10,000, k=1 and estimate=25/600000=0.0000416667 minutes, so wait. At E=50,000,000, k=2 and gap=62,500,000, estimate=104.1666667 minutes, so print if due. This discontinuity is the retained next-star rule. With zero income, show 'No positive sampled income' and do not print, even though the mathematical estimate tends to infinity. At E<12,500,000 the pending-star guard wins regardless of estimate.

It checks progression/Archive/retained materials, but ordinary invocation does not apply all challengeSettled/layerSettled checks.
