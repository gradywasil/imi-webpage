The card says: "Manually sell 1 authored book with only 2- and 3-letter recipe words within 10 real minutes. Publisher and away sales do not count. Reward: [exact N] bananas, fixed when you start. One successful brief per room run. Starting is free. Skipping or expiry has no penalty. This board does not create a pitch."

Eligibility uses recipe.band === 0 and !gen && !kid && !lib, not title-name length. The six current candidates are ape-in-the-cape, goodnight-baboon, very-hungry-capuchin, three-little-twigs, chimp-in-the-hat and green-eggs-and-hamster. Ops builds band from the longest token in reader text, dropping one-letter tokens. All six use Bamboo. Require at least one unsold, available candidate at Start. Show the available names and exact objective before Start. Stock may already exist; this is a sale objective, not proof that every ingredient was typed after Start. Archive withdrawal remains authoritative. Never replace a withdrawn book with generated/kids/library content.

Use the normal manual Sell button and actual current market salePay. The board does not reserve a title against Publisher or rewrite production sets. Tell the player to turn Publisher off if they want to save a qualifying title. If Publisher sells all eligible books, show "No eligible books remain this run. Abandon or continue after a reset." Starting while an ordinary challenge, mastery or A11 execution is active is unavailable. Starting while work or a save transaction is unsettled waits; existing work/PG preferences remain unchanged. Entering a challenge later ends an unfinished brief without penalty in the same acknowledged entry candidate. This avoids changing challenge sale/clear owners.

Start freezes the reviewed card amount and wall-time interval in one acknowledged candidate. Progress begins at 0/1. A qualifying acknowledged manual sale sets 1/1 and complete in the same sale successor. Complete exposes Claim; completion does not pay automatically. No second active brief until Claim. Claim is always available after completion, even after expiry/reset. No discard control is proposed for a completed reward. Abandon is only for unfinished briefs and pays nothing. Expired/abandoned briefs can restart free with a new sequence, new amount and full duration. Success consumes the room-run allowance at completion, even before Claim. Claim/reload cannot reopen it. A subsequent Printing, Imprint or Estate creates a new room allowance. No same-room successful replay; repeated play comes from normal resets, without a repeat reward farm on the same shelf. At most one completed unclaimed reward is retained, so Claim precedes another Start.

## Clock authority and exact proposal

README.md line 131 calls briefs timed in game time. Its pacing rule at line 160 excludes Commission expiries from game speed. execution.md line 14 explicitly retains wall-clock Commission expiries while assigning simulation production and Deadline to game time. Follow the execution decision. Do not rewrite either historical wording or existing Deadline/world/weather implementation in this task. The observed Deadline code still uses savedWallNow and away adjustments; this proposal makes no claim to repair or reclassify that implementation.

Recommended local clock: integer Date.now milliseconds. Freeze startedAt when the owner accepts the Start command, expiresAt = startedAt + 600000. Publication and the running interval require successful Start ACK. Initial Start save failure gives no active brief; same-process Retry keeps its frozen start time and amount, with an explicit "Start waiting to save; its reviewed deadline is [time]" notice. If Retry is already at/after that deadline, save the attempt as expired without starting or paying; a fresh Start requires another explicit review. Nothing durably frozen exists before ACK, and reload may lose that failed attempt.

Hidden, menu, hosted minigame, away, closed and reload time all consume the same interval. Game speed, typist speed, offline efficiency/caps and a save hold do not extend expiry. Use max(Date.now(), persisted lastObservedAt) whenever an acknowledged board command advances its clock. Backward local clock changes cannot reverse an observed expiry; unobserved clock changes while closed can alter the effective duration. No server/account, trusted-time or anti-cheat promise. Do not persist every display tick. Display quiet "Ends at [local date/time], including time away" and a status word, without a ticking countdown, flashing urgency, notification or exclusive offer. This gameplay deadline is unrelated to a commercial offer.

Eligibility requires sale acceptance time strictly less than expiresAt. Equality fails. Freeze acceptedAt only after owner/recipe/bank/run/board revalidation. A sale prepared before the deadline may finish/Retry after it: its durable acceptedAt proves timely acceptance. An unacknowledged same-process frozen sale may Retry exactly, but reload only recovers a durable preparation. Never invent a pre-deadline timestamp from a sold event, written map or away aggregate. A sale accepted at/after expiry may still sell normally, but cannot progress the brief. The board's expired status needs ACK before being called saved; display may truthfully say "Deadline passed, status waiting to save" while held. Completion already acknowledged before the boundary stays claimable forever. Expiry removes no ordinary income, inventory, awards or earned board reward.

## Reward choice and arithmetic

Recommend one banana C tier-0 payout, the smallest approved cash band. Proposed assignment to this recurring brief and first-Start locking require owner approval; approval of an award formula does not approve a new recurring consumer. No DB, new allocation, token, letter bundle or extra first/milestone reward. Keep all 68 awards/+1%, 109 existing DB, daily and first-clear claims unchanged.

Use current Ops rewardBasis().bananas, B = (royBase() + divBase()) * speed(). N = Economy.round(max(Economy.cash(250), min(Economy.cash(10000), 300 * B))). Economy.scale is 25. Floor 6250 actual bananas, cap 250000 actual bananas; round nearest 25, ties upward. B already uses actual bananas per real second and speed once. Never scale B by cash again. At B=100, N=30000; at B=0, N=6250; B=1000 reaches the 250000 cap. The 5-minute cash band is not the 10-minute objective duration. A 25-unit tie occurs at B=100.04166666666667, raw 30012.5, rounded 30025, subject to ordinary floating precision.

Sample at Start before any new sale or board payout, persist version/kind C/amount/rate/minutes=5/floor/cap/speed/origin and rule version. Preview is recomputed until reviewed Start, which revalidates the exact quote; if production changed, require a refreshed review rather than silently changing the displayed amount. It remains fixed through completion, claim, reset and Retry. Include permanent factors already in royBase/divBase; exclude sale bursts, reward income, pickups, daily wallet percentage, Rush/Critic/premiere temporary effects and recent-income samples. dailyPrize at ops.js:2720 is wallet-dependent, has streak multipliers and no matching tier cap; do not reuse it as the formula. Reward cash may legitimately increase current run.earned and ordinary wallet/Printing awards through existing credit accounting. It never counts as a title sale or board progress. No recursive board start/award sweep and no multiplier on the payout. Later runs can reflect changed sustained production within the cap; no claim-time resampling or automatic reinvestment loop is proposed.

## Actual progress owners and exclusions

ops.js:3344 writeTitle routes mastery and tracked pitch sales to named owners, then otherwise mutates live bank/written/stats, credits bananas and calls salePublished. salePublished at 3378 calls ordinary save and IMI.emit('sold',{id,pay}); save is not a durable acknowledgement. Quiet away sales skip that ordinary save and may use awayCredit aggregate checkpoints. The event carries neither source identity nor durable receipt and may be published again during recovery. Never subscribe to it for board progress or infer a delta from written counts.

Existing A11 challengeAction/continueChallenge in rewards.js and challengeCommit in Ops prepare, creditReward, then ACK a detached successor with bank/written/stats/run/receipt and sixth-sale clear before salePublished. Mastery and tracked pitch use separate named owners. These are patterns to preserve, not permission to merge their transactions or count their presentation callbacks.

First slice counting is deliberately narrower than the master's five-title example:

- Ordinary visible manual authored band-0 sale, while a supported active brief exists and no challenge/work conflict: new named board-sale owner, count exactly once in its ACK successor. Production may come from real manual presses, crew/keepers or retained stock. PG collection/manual/advance and Archive availability still govern normal production.
- Publisher online, silent/away Publisher, A11 clerk and mastery sales: zero board progress. Preserve ordinary income/challenge behavior. No new consumers in those owners. Manual takeover from A11 first follows existing acknowledged handover and ends the conflicting unfinished board on challenge entry, rather than dual ownership.
- Generated pitches, including tracked manual/Publisher predecessor and A7: excluded even if band 0. Their receipt and pitchSold behavior remain unchanged.
- Kids/library: excluded; ordinary progression already excludes them from soldCount. They remain useful ordinary sales, never title proof here.
- Folio binds/reorders, Archive deposits/retirement/income, Works demo/material/signatures, minigame payouts, awards/daily claims: excluded. No invented sold event, title-count substitute or extra payout.
- Already written, pre-Start, stale run/sequence, duplicate, withdrawn or at/after-expiry sales: no progress. A reset ends an unfinished attempt; no cross-room reuse of its title proof.

Only the selected manual-sale branch gains a new consumer. The real prerequisite is an acknowledged ordinary authored sale owner; it does not exist today. Without it, ship no claimable brief. Do not broaden into all-sale receipts, generic quests or a transaction rewrite. Owner may reject manual-only scope, but then a new source/design lease must name each additional sale owner before implementation.

## Bounded optional state and exact-once delivery

Propose optional meta.commissionBoard version 1 with ruleVersion, access, sequence, roomSequence, roomSucceeded, active-or-null, lastOutcome, lastClaim and pending-or-null. active contains run ID `board:<sequence>`, roomSequence, brief ID, status active/complete, startedAt/expiresAt/lastObservedAt, fixed descriptor and at most one title proof. lastOutcome is one bounded summary. lastClaim is one bounded monetary receipt. sequence and roomSequence are nonnegative safe integers, checked before increment; no wrap. Preserve unknown fields recursively. No unbounded recurring entries in rewards.claims or prem.credits. Existing finite reward row()/validateDescriptor accepts named awards/firsts only; it cannot accept arbitrary recurring IDs today. Add a fixed named board descriptor validator/coordinator, not a caller-chosen reward API.

Room sequence is board-local, created at first successful Start. It does not create Estate/resetEpoch or change lifetime layers.count. Every successful room replacement increments an existing board's roomSequence and clears roomSucceeded in that same detached candidate, even when board UI is disabled. It closes an unfinished attempt with reset outcome; complete/unclaimed descriptor and receipt survive. Printing/Imprint/Estate currently retain meta and rewards, but retained bytes alone do not implement this rule. Add one idempotent board reset projection called by ordinary Printing, acknowledged Printing/A10/A11 entry and Layers.projectRoom used for Imprint/Estate. It must run only once per successful replacement, never on retries or render. No board namespace means no added fields. Challenge entry closes only an unfinished attempt, leaving room allowance unchanged. No active brief can veto ordinary layer progress. Pending sale/claim reconciliation retains the existing short transaction hold, not a new gameplay reset gate.

Start/abandon/expire use detached Ops ACK before publishing. Board sale command accepts only title ID and expected board/run identity, never caller amount/candidate/progress. Revalidate real recipe/availability/canWrite, no prior written, ordinary restrictions, current board, clock and settled owner. Freeze bank debits/pay/market/source identity and monetary sequence. ACK baseline then prepared sale; credit the ordinary sale price once through existing Core creditReward; ACK successor with bank debit, written/stats/focus cleanup/run delta, walletHead, title proof and complete/roomSucceeded. Publish sale effects afterward. Keep ordinary sale price and board reward in separate receipt identities. This path never calls legacy writeTitle after payment, and never pays the sale a second time at Claim. No new Core monetary authority is requested.

Claim ID `board-claim:<sequence>` is independent of amount/rule version. Under existing Rewards writer exclusion, baseline ACK, persist prepared fixed descriptor and next wallet sequence/previous marker/current room context, creditReward once, then ACK board claimed outcome/lastClaim plus current run delta and walletHead. Preserve the preceding wallet-head chain, including pitch/mastery/A11/Archive/away origins. A paid wallet with missing successor finalizes once without credit again; a predecessor marker retries fixed payment; unexplained markers or malformed/unknown versions hold without deletion. Retain receipt until a later acknowledged wallet head safely succeeds it; monotonic board sequence refuses stale claims after bounded compaction. No reset or ordinary writer bypasses a pending command. No stale run snapshot restoration.

Failed first prepare retains only a volatile exact command and pays nothing. Durable pending Retry/reload finishes that exact command before new clock/expiry/reset work, then evaluates the next state. Claim after completion has no deadline. Successor failure cannot erase completion or issue another payout. Startup must recognize supported board pending/receipt forms before Core, away, default or flagless writers. Missing opt-in/dependencies preserves raw keys and offers the established recovery gate, rather than running legacy code over new state. A dormant settled board without its flag preserves data; reset/challenge projection still closes/increments supported state where needed. Unknown state requires supported recovery, not normalization to empty. Settings full reset follows the existing timeline-deletion restrictions; no new erase/recovery promise. External rollback/deletion and unacknowledged process death retain current local-save limits, not account-level exactly-once.
