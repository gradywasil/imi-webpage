## 2. The Chimpest

UNAPPROVED. Prospero learns to ask his island troupe what show they want after a homemade storm scatters his invitations.

### Canonical body the-chimpest

```text
Prospero made a rainstorm with drums, buckets, and one sheet. His island stage needed guests. Sadly, his invitations had blown into the lagoon.

"I ordered soft winds!" he cried.

Ariel the gibbon shook another coconut. Miranda caught the sheet before it ate their ship. Inside, Gonzalo applauded the weather. He said the trip meant washday.

Caliban emerged beneath the stage, with every missing invitation as a hat.

"Nobody asked before using my beach," he said.

Prospero lowered his staff. The blue waves grew still. Even Ariel hung, swaying upside down beside a paper cloud.

Miranda asked everyone what island show they wanted. Caliban chose crab dancers. Gonzalo wanted chairs without splinters. Ariel chose to play tunes and leave those rain buckets alone.

Prospero listened, then gave each ape a pencil. Together they drew a new ending, with room for every suggestion.

That night, Caliban greeted the guests. Ariel played flute while Miranda steered the ship across the sand. Prospero played the smallest crab, because someone had lost his best costume.

He shuffled sideways, delighted.

When the guests demanded another rainstorm, Gonzalo rose and shook his socks. A sock landed on the ship flag.

Everyone agreed the storm was much better.
```

## 3. Groomio and Juliet

UNAPPROVED. Groomio and Juliet unite rival flower clubs after a parrot mangles a perfectly friendly planting message.

### Canonical body groomio-and-juliet

```text
Two garden clubs divided Veronan Vines. Monteapes planted purple flowers. Capuchulets preferred yellow. Nobody remembered why the other color was wrong.

Groomio met Juliet beside the fence. She balanced a water jug on her tail. He offered support and received a surprise shower.

"Your flowers look thirsty," she said.

"My ears were thirstier," he said.

They planted a striped sunflower in a pot. Friar Lemur lent them seeds, while Nurse carried messages between their gardens.

Sadly, their parrot cut each message short. "Plant beside the gate" became "Ban the gate!" Both clubs arrived with mallets and offended faces.

Juliet stood on a plant pot. Groomio held it steady. They explained their plan, but the parrot kept shouting about a grand gate feast.

Then Nurse unfolded the first note. Everyone read it. No gates were banned.

The clubs lowered mallets and built a shared trellis instead. They planted purple flowers beside yellow ones, with the sunflower standing between them like a very cheery judge.

When it grew tall, its petals were bright yellow. Its stem leaned over both gardens.

"Even flowers ignore fences," she joked.

The parrot considered this, then said, "Ignore sandwiches!"

Nobody took that advice. Gardening made monkeys hungry.
```

```text

Iago returned the glove and apologized. They shared conducting turns. Emilia wrote the order where everybody could read it.

For the finale, Iago waved the baton carefully. The beetles joined on cymbals. Desdemona rang her bells, Cashew thundered, and Ookthello wore a paper hat.

It held no snacks, but it made an excellent trumpet mute. The crowd cheered with both paws.

He apologized and invited his daughters to plan the canopy fairly. They chose places for music, dancing, gardens, and quiet naps. Coralia wanted everybody consulted, including the smallest monkeys.

The Fool received a silent bell, which he rang with tremendous dignity.

Lemur finally slept. Above him hung a sign asking guests to admire his grand whiskers very quietly.
```

Yorick played the king that night. Clawdius moved the scenery. The old king became the ghost, now with proper consent.

Chimplet bowed beside his friends. The royal chair squeaked again.

The troupe gave it the final line.
