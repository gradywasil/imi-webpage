## Goal

A player who plays 30-60 minutes a day (plus idle and offline time) should still find new things to do **after a month**. Every "bigger picture" loop takes **at least two or three days** to get through. The game is **ad-free forever**; income comes from optional Diamond Bananas that also trickle out free through achievements, so a long-time free player eventually owns some premium upgrades.

## Where the game is today (measured, not guessed)

- One real layer of meta-progression: **Second Printing** (`starsNow() = floor(sqrt(run.earned / cash(5e5)))`), Legacy stars spent on 5 upgrades (`LEG`), 4 Oulipo challenges whose perks are small stat bonuses.
- 68 awards (24 bronze, 25 silver, 19 gold), each worth +1% income. No other reward.
- Playtest bot: second machine at 13.7-17.3 min active; no idle seed reaches it in the first hour; ~1.2M real bananas earned in an active first hour. The first prestige star needs 12.5M real bananas of run earnings. **Measured in LG-00:** the Regular seed-1 policy reaches authored titles around day 5, six desks around day 6.33, full Legacy around day 8.67 and its declared finite cap endpoint around day 21, with 21 active hours. These are distinct milestones under one documented policy, not universal player durations or continuous new unlocks through day 21. This supersedes the original untested one-to-two-day estimate. The free Regular six-week target remains unchanged; later layers must add varied decisions rather than multiply this cap-maxing duration into more grind.
- Automation that exists: keepers (free), Hold to type, Publisher's assistant (3 tiers), Night Lamp (offline cap 2 h base, 4/8/16 h with the lamp tiers). Everything else is manual: hiring, coaching, shop buying, restoring, planting, pitching, releasing, seating Muses, collecting pickups, printing.
- Word lengths top out at 13 letters and six machines. **A long game cannot be built only by adding bigger numbers on those axes**; each new layer needs one genuinely new mechanic (below).

## Design principles

1. **Each loop: do it by hand, beat a hard version of it, automate it, then the next layer gives you a new thing to do by hand.** That is the Cookie Clicker / Revolution Idle rhythm. Automation is *earned in the game*, never sold.
2. **A reset must be worth it the first time you do it.** Layer N's reset currency is never shown as "number go up by 2%". It always unlocks a visible new thing on the first reset.
3. **Reset currency uses a sub-linear formula** (sqrt or log, like `starsNow`). Doubling income then gives about x1.4 reset currency, not x2. This is the main defence against speed boosts collapsing the timeline (see "Pacing and premium").
4. **Mechanics-first, numbers-second.** New layer = new axis (a new resource, a new constraint, a new kind of decision), not a larger multiplier.
5. **Respect the player's time.** Offline progress stays generous, no daily-login punishment, no forced waiting that a payment skips.
6. **Mechanics never silently change under existing saves.** Old saves load; new fields are optional with defaults (same rule as the art track).

## New objectives and content that keep the loops fed

1. **Reward engine** (see below): every award and challenge/layer first-clear pays something. Goals direct to those payouts rather than paying a second reward.
2. **Goal strip**: three stable next goals chosen from unfinished awards after intro completion and Awards reveal. Phones use one 44px three-summary button at normal text size in the visible ticker slot, with owner-approved growth for enlarged text and full goals in Awards; desktop keeps the ticker. During reward-save holds, existing effect/challenge chips sit inside the Retry panel between message and button. Local both-edition fit is accepted at the three required sizes; physical/native zoom remains unverified. See LG-01-accepted.md.
3. **Commission board**: timed (in *game* time) briefs, e.g. "deliver 5 titles of 6 letters or fewer in 10 minutes"; recurring commissions pay ordinary gameplay rewards. Any later DB assignment must be an explicitly allocated once-only progression milestone within the finite pool.
4. **Oulipo expansion**: 4 -> 12 constraints with bronze/silver/gold clears. Each constraint gives a real automation or tool when first cleared, not just a stat.
5. **Hall of Monkeys**: a collection book for the 30 looks and 4 rarities; completing sets pays rewards. Costs art already made.
6. **Seasons** (wall-clock, weekly): a rotating theme (genre week, storm week) that pays *extra*; skipping a week costs nothing but the extra. Not affected by game speed.
7. **Awards 68 -> ~250**: written per layer (about 60 per layer), because awards are the unit of reward and of Diamond Banana income.

## Reward engine (the achievement payouts)

Every award, challenge first-clear and layer first-clear pays one **reward** assigned from four kinds. Goals point to the same award reward without duplicate payouts. The reward is **shown on the card before it is earned** (no mystery boxes).

| Kind | What it is | Scaling rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Letters | a bundle of letters on the best desk you own, optionally a requested letter | 3-10 min of current letter output, floor 50 |
| Bananas | a banana payout | 5-30 min of current income, rounded to the 25-banana denomination, floor `cash(250)` (like `dailyPrize`) |
| Golden banana | a stored golden banana you pop when you like (buff) | fixed |
| Diamond Bananas | premium currency | fixed per tier: bronze 0, silver 0-2, gold 3-5; layer and challenge firsts 5-50 |

**Decided (Q1):** the "regular bananas" in the owner's brief means golden bananas, so the four reward kinds are letters, bananas, golden bananas and Diamond Bananas.

Scaling by "minutes of current income" means a bronze award is never worthless late and never breaks the early game. All existing awards keep their +1% income and gain a reward; none of the 68 existing awards is removed or renamed (players keep what they have).

**LG-01 owner-approved detail, 2026-10-12:** the [reward design](LG-01-reward-design.md) defines the exact 68-row assignments, bounded sustained-production formulas, 109-DB existing-content pool, DB-only historical backfill and fixed stored x7 royalty/division rush for 15 game seconds. Recurring daily/Season/commission rewards do not grant DB; the free progression pool stays finite. Goals reveal progressively and do not pay twice. Startup writer exclusion and acknowledged reward persistence require the narrow Core/Economy and edition-boot scope described there. Amounts and caps are approved for implementation and must still pass enabled economy tests; no runtime acceptance or future price approval is implied.

## Pacing and premium: the one real risk

A premium **x2 game speed** plus **x2 typist speed** will shorten a player's *calendar* time to the end. That is the opposite of "play for a month". Defences, all required:

1. Calibrate the free path to **about six weeks** for the Regular profile. Then a fully boosted player still needs about three weeks and sees all content.
2. Sub-linear reset currencies (principle 3). Typist speed only multiplies *letters*; the bottleneck later is words, titles and decisions, so it saturates.
3. Game speed multiplies simulated time, but **not** the world clock, weather, daily crate, Seasons or Commission board expiries.
4. Acceptance test (LG-12): a **maxed-premium bot** must take **at least 55%** of the free bot's calendar days to reach the capstone on the same profile, and **at least 80%** to clear each of layers 2 and 3.

Full premium numbers: [premium-currency.md](premium-currency.md).

## Technical shape

- `ops.js` is one 2,700-line IIFE and the art track also needs it. **Do not keep adding to it.** LG-00 creates extension seams (a small hook table inside `ops.js` that new files register into, plus `IMI.ops.dev` already exposed) and the new systems live in new files: `rewards.js`, `automation.js`, `layers.js` (imprint/estate), `vault.js` (premium UI). Each is a plain `<script>` (no build tooling) added to `index.html`, `classic.html`, `tools/balance.html` and `sw.js`.
- Save: new fields live under `S.meta` (layers, automation levels), `S.rewards` (claimed ids) and `S.prem` (Diamond Bananas, owned catalogue). All optional with defaults; no migration may drop data. `economyVersion` bumps only for deliberate re-pricing.
- **Game speed hook** is a single `speed()` multiplier applied once, to `dt` in `tick()`, to `simulateAway()`, and to the garden/premiere/pickup clocks. Default 1; with it at 1 the balance harness must be byte-identical.
- Backend only for payments (premium spec). The game remains a static site: Cloudflare Worker + KV/D1 for receipts, nothing else.
